# Code of conduct

Medfold adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive. Focus on what is best for the people using
Medfold. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else in the project's spaces.

Because Medfold deals with health records, one more rule: **never post real medical documents or
anyone's health information** in issues or pull requests. Use the synthetic samples in
`tests/sample-pdfs/`.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@n3h4a](https://github.com/n3h4a), on
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer will follow the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary or permanent ban).
