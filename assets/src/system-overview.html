Medfold: your medical history in one place

Upload a report or a phone photo. Medfold reads it, organises it, and lines up every result over time.

The problem

A folder of paper, carried to every doctor

  • Reports from different labs, different formats and units
  • No way to see a value change over time
  • Lost pages, faded prints, repeat tests
  • Problem severity 9/10, ITCH score 73.5 (Razorpay)
